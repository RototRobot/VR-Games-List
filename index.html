FAQ

Q: Why should I listen to you?

A: Really, I'm no one special, I just play a lot of VR.

Q: A lot of these games look indie or AA were are the AAA games?

as of the time of making list there are only about 8 million Active Quest users and 4-5 SteamVR users. it important to understand that VR is still quite niche and that only a handful of AAA developers have invested. but VR is still changing rapidly and Indies are making some really creative games that I think are worth looking at too.

Q: I've already played everything you listed!

A: Well then this list might not be for you, it's really meant for people just getting into VR or on the fence not a verteran like yourself.
